## OkHttp Public Suffix Database

The Android extension redistributes the unmodified `publicsuffixes.gz` resource
from OkHttp 4.12.0 because Defold's Android extender does not copy arbitrary
classpath resources from Maven JARs into the final APK.

Source: https://repo.maven.apache.org/maven2/com/squareup/okhttp3/okhttp/4.12.0/
